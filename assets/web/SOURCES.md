# Web-sourced photography

Everything else on this site is the brand's own photography. These five frames
are not — they dress the "Our house" story section, which needed editorial
imagery (a spice table, a shared plate) that the brand's asset library does not
contain.

Every file here is from **Pexels**, under the [Pexels
License](https://www.pexels.com/license/): free for commercial use, no
attribution required, modification allowed. They are credited anyway.

None of these photographs shows Bapu Best product, packaging, staff or premises,
and none is captioned as though it did — see the `alt` text in
`src/components/HouseSection.tsx`. Replace them with the family's own
photographs whenever those exist; only `assets.manifest.mjs` and this folder
have to change.

## A note on the five process frames

"How we make it" used to be drawn rather than photographed, for a reason worth
restating: a stock photograph of somebody else's factory, captioned as this
kitchen, is a manufacturing claim nobody can evidence.

The photographs are here now because the business asked for them, and they were
chosen so that the original reason still holds. Each one shows the *craft* and
not this kitchen — a scoop of pulses, a kadai, chilli on a spoon, a food hall, a
plain unbranded pouch under a sealing machine. No frame shows a Bapu Best pack,
premises or person, no alt text says it does, and the note printed under the
section tells the reader in plain words that these are illustrative. Photographs
of the Phalka Bazar kitchen should replace all five the day they exist.
